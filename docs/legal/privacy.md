# Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information {{COMPANY}} ("we") collects through {{PRODUCT}}, why, who it's shared
with, and the choices you have. It's written to describe exactly how the Service works today.

## The short version

- Much of {{PRODUCT}} works without an account, and what you make is kept **in your own browser**.
- With an account, we store your email address and what you choose to save or share with your team.
- There are **no ads and no ad trackers**, and we **don't sell or rent personal information**.
- To answer what you ask about a place, the Service fetches maps, weather and other public data, and those
  providers see the request (for example, the coordinates you're looking at) and your IP address.

## 1. Information we collect

**Account information.** Your email address, and a session token kept on your device so you stay signed in.

**What you save.** If you're signed in: your public page if you make one (name, handle, places and whatever you
write), lenses and guides you publish, private items that sync between your devices (field notes, watches), and
team workspaces you create or join (for example sites, facilities, schools, visit notes), with who saved each
version and when.

**What stays on your device.** Without an account, and for anything you don't sync, your places, projects and
workspaces are stored in your browser's local storage. We can't see it. Clearing your browser data deletes it,
so back it up (My Place › Your data, or Team › Download a backup).

**Feedback.** If you send feedback, we receive what you write and, if you choose to include them, recent error
messages from your device.

**Technical information.** Like any website, our hosting and edge providers receive your IP address, browser
type and the pages requested, and keep standard logs for security and reliability. We don't use advertising
cookies or cross-site tracking. If we add privacy-friendly usage statistics (counts of pages and features used,
without cookies or personal profiles), we'll list them in section 4.

**Camera features.** Camera analysis in My Place runs on your device; video isn't sent to us.

## 2. How we use it

To provide the Service (sign-in, saving and syncing, sharing with the people you invite); to keep it secure and
working (preventing abuse, fixing errors); to communicate with you about your account and important changes; and
to improve the Service using aggregate, non-identifying information. We don't use your content to train AI
models, and we don't make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

## 3. Who sees it

- **People you choose.** Your public page, published lenses and guides are public. Team workspaces are visible to
  the members you invite, in the roles you give them.
- **Service providers** that run the Service for us, under contracts that limit them to doing so. They're listed
  on our [subprocessors page](subprocessors.html).
- **Public data providers.** When you look at a place, the Service asks map, search, weather and other public
  services about it. They receive the request and your IP address, not your account details. Where our edge
  service is switched on, many of these requests pass through it instead, so the provider sees our server, not
  you. The list is on the subprocessors page.
- **Atlas AI.** If you turn on Atlas AI, the requests you type are sent to Anthropic (Claude) through our proxy
  to be answered. Anthropic doesn't use API data to train its models by default.
- **The law.** We'll disclose information if the law requires it, and we'll tell you first unless we're not
  allowed to. If our business is sold or merged, your information may transfer to the new owner under this
  policy, and we'll tell you.

## 4. Analytics and cookies

The Service stores what it needs in your browser's local storage (your settings, saved work, sign-in session).
It doesn't set advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Usage statistics, if enabled: none yet.

## 5. How long we keep it

Account information and saved content: while your account is open. Team workspace versions: the latest 200 per
workspace. After you delete your account or a workspace's owner deletes it, we delete the data within 30 days,
and backups within a further 30 days. Server logs: up to 30 days. We may keep information longer where the law
requires it.

## 6. Your choices and rights

You can view, correct, download and delete what you've saved from inside the Service, or ask us at
{{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. Depending on where you live (for example California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Maryland
and other US states, or the UK and EU), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal
information, and to opt out of its sale or targeted advertising (we do neither). We'll answer within the time
the law allows and won't treat you differently for asking. If you're in the EU or UK and have a concern, you can
also contact your data protection authority.

**Information in an organization's workspace** (for example a district's staff list or a union's visit notes)
is controlled by that organization. Ask them first; we'll help them answer.

## 7. Children and students

The Service isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. The
education tools are for teachers and staff. Schools and districts must not put students' personal information
into the Service until they've signed our Data Processing Addendum with its student-data terms (FERPA and state
student-privacy laws). If you believe a child has given us personal information, tell us and we'll delete it.

## 8. Security

We use encryption in transit, database access rules that only let each person reach what they're allowed to,
and providers with independent security certifications. See our [security overview](security.html). No system
is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your information, we'll tell you as the law requires.

## 9. Where it's stored

Our database is hosted in the United States. If you use the Service from elsewhere, your information is
transferred to and processed in the US, under safeguards required by law where they apply.

## 10. Changes

We'll post updates here and, for material changes, tell you by email or in the Service before they take effect.
