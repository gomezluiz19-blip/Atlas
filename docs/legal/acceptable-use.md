# Acceptable Use Policy

This policy is part of the [Terms of Service](terms.html). It applies to everyone who uses {{PRODUCT}}.

## Don't use the Service to

1. **Break the law**, or help someone else break it.
2. **Surveil, track or profile people** without a lawful basis, including following someone's movements,
   compiling information to harass or intimidate them, or doxxing.
3. **Interfere with workers' rights.** The construction tools serve contractors, unions, suppliers and planners
   alike. No one may use them to monitor, record, identify or report on workers' protected concerted activity
   (such as union organizing, membership or meetings), to blacklist workers, or to retaliate against anyone for
   such activity. Union workspaces and contractor workspaces are separate, and neither side can see the other's.
4. **Discriminate** unlawfully, for example in housing, employment, lending or access to public services.
5. **Upload content you don't have the right to share**, malware, or content that's unlawful, defamatory,
   threatening or sexually exploitative.
6. **Put sensitive personal information where it doesn't belong**: health records, Social Security numbers,
   financial account numbers, precise information about children, or students' personal information (unless your
   organization has signed our Data Processing Addendum with student-data terms).
7. **Attack or overload the Service**: probe or test its security without our written permission (see our
   [security page](security.html) for responsible disclosure), get around access controls or rate limits, or
   scrape it at scale.
8. **Misuse the data sources** the Service shows, against their own terms (for example bulk downloading imagery).
9. **Resell or sublicense the Service**, or use it to build a competing product, without our agreement.
10. **Pretend to be someone else**, or misrepresent your connection to an organization.
11. **Use Terreno AI** to produce content that breaks this policy or Anthropic's usage policies.
